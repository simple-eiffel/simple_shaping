## THE LRU-ORDER DECISION (LAYOUT_CACHE)

cache_model (key -> layout map) is THE abstract state; LRU recency is
deliberately NOT part of it (design decision 05, kept). keys_model (LRU order,
oldest first) is retained as a specification WITNESS, not abstract state:
eviction and rebound postconditions name their victims through it
(put.evict_is_exact / lru_victim_gone use (old keys_model).first;
set_capacity.newest_survive uses its tail), and item_verified may reorder it
on a hit - the one lawful benign effect, now stated positively
(recency_touched_on_hit) and framed on the miss path (no_touch_on_miss).
Frames are stated over cache_model always, over keys_model only where the LRU
discipline itself is the promise. Documented in the class note.
