REPORTED CONTRACT CHANGE (gate decision 2)
==========================================
ONE clause was added, to FONT_REGISTRY.font:

    realized_on_first_use: Result.is_realization_attempted

ADDED, never a rewrite: every pre-existing clause of `font' stands verbatim
(machine-checked below).

Reason it is this clause and NOT `ready: Result.is_ready':
  Decision 2 pre-authorizes the strengthening "if realization-on-first-use
  makes it true". Realization-on-first-use makes `is_ready' true on a machine
  with a working GDI - not universally. `is_ready' is the answer of a NATIVE
  operation that a machine may refuse: CreateFontIndirectW returns NULL,
  CreateCompatibleDC returns NULL, GetTextMetricsW fails. GDI32_API's own note
  binds the library to "failures surface as default_pointer/False plus notes
  upstream, never exceptions" (NFR-011), and `layout' is contracted as a total
  function. A postcondition `Result.is_ready' would convert exactly those
  failures into an assertion violation escaping `layout' through
  registry.font - the one thing NFR-011 forbids - and would simultaneously
  declare the facade's own `if font.is_ready then ... else degrade' branch
  unreachable, so the degradation path would be dead code until the day it
  was not.
  `is_realization_attempted' is the part that IS unconditionally true after
  `font' returns, and it is the part that was actually missing: the Phase-1
  body handed back an untouched identity, and no contract said the registry
  realizes anything. The new clause makes "the registry realizes" statable
  without promising the machine's answer. Callers read `is_ready' and degrade.

  LARRY'S GATE, if he wants the stronger form: changing
  `realized_on_first_use' to `ready: Result.is_ready' is a one-line edit here
  and in FONT_REGISTRY's note. It is NOT taken on my own judgment, and the
  reason above is the one to weigh against it.

No other require / ensure / invariant clause was added, modified, weakened,
strengthened or removed anywhere, and no existing feature signature changed.
